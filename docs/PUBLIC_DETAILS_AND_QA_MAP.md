## Stale-note example and follow-up

Songs Exact!G92/H92 contain `2019-11-08` at day precision, while W92 still says the
earlier single day is not established. Sources row 588 (`src-039`) records an Apple
Music source with the original single date, and V92 still carries an older
year-only supported claim. This is a strong reconciliation candidate. No claim or
uncertainty was silently deleted, and no independent source check is claimed.

The map flags 42 possible exact-date/older-uncertainty combinations for review.
They are not 42 automatically disproven notes: some can legitimately distinguish
an edition date from the original release. Preserve working notes until the
relevant evidence and record are reconciled.

## Presentation changes

- Remove review banners/links, raw Basis/uncertainty/provenance paragraphs and
  technical precision labels from public artist/song pages. Public content, REST
  representations and song RSS use the reader presentation rather than expose
  the original working research body.
- Keep approved About text, artist/reporting/editorial attribution, references,
  recording credits, tags, photo licenses/modification credits and Spotify privacy
  disclosures. Removing QA does not remove source accountability.
- Show release date/title/format as reader details, respecting stored year/month/
  day precision. Do not invent an exact day. Do not silently recast an edition
  date as the first-ever release.
- Use About for default song description/social metadata; explicit editor SEO
  descriptions remain authoritative. Keep stored excerpts unchanged.
- Keep custom artist biographies and public formatting; remove only known
  importer-generated QA headings/paragraphs from their presentation.
- In WordPress **Edit artist/song → Internal research and QA**, show the retained
  Basis, release context, uncertainties, supported claims, approval/provenance,
  source status and original content. The panel has no save/migration handler.
  Authenticated editor REST context=edit also keeps the original content.raw.

No post fields, metadata, IDs, URLs, statuses, approvals, source access statuses,
indexing settings or workbook cells were changed. The public catalog remains ten
artists / 128 songs. Removing visible notes is not a claim that open research is
resolved. Production indexing/deployment remains a separate release step.
